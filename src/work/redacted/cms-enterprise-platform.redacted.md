<h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>

<p>
  The project was a high-traffic national healthcare web platform serving beneficiaries, healthcare providers, researchers, and the press. The existing platform had accumulated long-term technical debt and inconsistent information architecture.
</p>

<h2 id="challenge">The Challenge</h2>

<p>
  The platform served multiple distinct audiences with different needs and mental models. The previous design tried to serve everyone equally and resulted in poor task completion for key audiences.
</p>

<h2 id="approach">Approach</h2>

<p>
  We began with a research phase including interviews across key audience types, a content audit of a large site, and analysis of search patterns and failure modes.
</p>
<p>
  The design decision was to use progressive disclosure to route users to tailored experiences based on role and task.
</p>

<h2 id="results">Results</h2>

<p>
  The redesign launched on schedule. Early metrics showed improved task completion rates and a measurable reduction in support volume. Specific figures have been removed.
</p>
