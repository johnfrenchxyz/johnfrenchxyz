<h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>

<p>
  The digital collections portal holds a very large number of items across many formats. The existing discovery experience had not been substantially redesigned for many years.
</p>

<h2 id="challenge">The Challenge</h2>

<p>
  Users ranged from professional researchers to the general public. The challenge was designing a search and browse experience that served power users and new users alike.
</p>

<h2 id="design">Design Approach</h2>

<p>
  We conducted contextual inquiries with subject-matter experts and usability studies with public users. Key insights informed a "search wide, filter narrow" model and a rebuilt faceted filtering system.
</p>

<h2 id="impact">Impact</h2>

<p>
  The redesigned search improved engagement and session depth. Exact metrics and organization-specific details have been removed from this public draft.
</p>
