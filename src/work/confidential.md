<h2 id="context">Context</h2>

<p>
  This engagement involved leading the product design function for a rapidly scaling Series B SaaS company in the enterprise workflow space. Due to an active NDA, specific company and product details are omitted.
</p>

<h2 id="process">Process</h2>

<p>
  We began with a full design audit and competitive landscape analysis. Within the first 8 weeks, I had established a design system, hired two additional designers, and established weekly design review rituals with the product and engineering leadership.
</p>
<p>
  The core design work focused on three problem areas: onboarding (time-to-value was over two weeks for new customers), a 0→1 collaboration feature, and a complete redesign of the primary workflow surface.
</p>

<h2 id="outcomes">Outcomes</h2>

<p>
  The redesigned onboarding reduced time-to-value from 14 days to under 3 days. The collaboration feature launched to positive reception in beta. Design system adoption reached 100% of new feature work within 6 months.
</p>
